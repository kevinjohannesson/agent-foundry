How it works
  • Hub is the controller for one workstream. Drag a proposal from its inventory into the area to approve it. Set it to Auto and it places them for you.
  • Batches: a chain runs tickets in order in one spot. An array runs a big queue on several sessions at once; more cells finish sooner.
  • Bots are short-lived sessions. They fly out and back without asking you.
  • Envelopes are messages. Filled: report to the controller. White: instruction from it. Outlined: one session to another.
  • Belts carry PRs. Ring colour is the target: white main, pink candidate, split both. Piles are stacks.
  • Train is the scheduled merge task. It brings ready PRs to the rocket silo, which launches them to the remote.
  • Chest keeps archived sessions and their handoffs. Restore puts one back.
